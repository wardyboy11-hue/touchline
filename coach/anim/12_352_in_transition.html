Getting the voice ready

For parents: what to look for

Blue is us in a 3-5-2. One sitting midfielder and two ahead make a triangle. Watch how we stay compact out of possession, open up in possession, and react in the first five seconds of a transition.

Set up

  • Full pitch. Us in a 3-5-2 facing the right goal.
  • Midfield triangle: one CDM sitting, two CMs ahead.
  • Wing backs hold the width. Two strikers stay high.
  • Opposition start with the ball in midfield.

Rules

  • Out of possession: pack the middle, force play wide, then press the channel and cut it off.
  • In possession: triangle stays connected, wing backs get wide, strikers pin the centre backs.
  • On every turnover: first five seconds decide it. Secure, support, spring.

How it starts and restarts

  • We start in our 3-5-2. Ball with the opposition midfielder.
  • Reset to the shape after each phase.
  • Same eleven, same triangle.

Coaching points

  • CDM sits. Do not chase. You are the lid on the box.
  • Two CMs form the triangle with him. Stay connected.
  • Pack the middle so they have to go wide.
  • Press the channel, then cut the ball off when it moves.
  • Transition both ways in the first five seconds. That is the game.

Progressions

  • Add a condition: first goal after a turnover counts double.
  • Limit the CDM to three touches so the CMs have to rotate.
