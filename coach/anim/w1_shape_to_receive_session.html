Getting the voice ready

For parents and players: what to look for

Watch how the players move before the ball arrives: someone shows short, others give width, and the first pass goes forward.

Warm up: Open, Look, Forward rondo

5v2 rondo, 14m x 14m box

Set up

  • 5 blues around a 14m box, 2 reds in the middle.
  • Swap the reds every 60 seconds.

Rules

  • Two touches.
  • A pass that splits the two reds scores a point.

How it starts

  • Number 4 starts with the ball on the edge of the box
  • Ball out or won: the red who won it swaps with the blue who lost it.

Coaching points

  • Open your body: back foot to the ball.
  • Look before it arrives, two looks.
  • Pass through the middle when it is on.

Make it harder

  • One touch for a split pass.
  • Make the box smaller, 12m.

Practice 1: Through the middle: three zones

6v3 across three zones, 36m x 24m, three zones of 12m

Set up

  • Three zones of 12m. Two blues in each end zone, two blues and two reds in the middle, one red in one end zone.

Rules

  • The ball must go through the middle zone.
  • Middle player: receive and play forward in two touches for a point.

How it starts

  • Number 4 starts with the ball in the end zone
  • Reds win it: play it out to the coach and go again.
  • Rotate the middle players every 2 minutes.

Coaching points

  • Check away, then show.
  • Receive side on, on the back foot.
  • First look forward, then spring beyond.

Make it harder

  • Add a third red in the middle.
  • One touch in the end zones.

Practice 2: Out from the back, find the wing back, then forward

10v5 phase of play to goal, Half pitch, 50m x 46m

Set up

  • Half pitch with a full size goal. Our keeper and ten against five reds and their keeper.
  • Two small gates at our end for the reds to counter into.

Rules

  • Restart from our keeper each time.
  • Reds win it: they can score in either small gate.

How it starts

  • Our keeper (1) starts with the ball
  • Ball out: back to our keeper.

Coaching points

  • Back three: split wide early.
  • Wing backs: width and height.
  • First look forward, then spring.

Make it harder

  • Add a sixth red to press.
  • Centre backs have two touches.

The game: Show, Support, Stretch, Spring

11v7 shape game on a big pitch, 70m x 46m, a goal at each end

Set up

  • Our eleven in a 3-5-2 against six outfield players and a keeper.
  • Halfway marked with cones.

Rules

  • Restart from our keeper each time.
  • A goal from a forward pass in the first three passes counts double.

How it starts

  • Our keeper (1) starts with the ball
  • Ball out: back to our keeper.

Coaching points

  • Show early, before the ball is played.
  • Open your body to face forward.
  • Wing backs: width and height.
  • First look is forward.

Make it harder

  • Opponents press with three.
  • Limit our centre backs to two touches.
