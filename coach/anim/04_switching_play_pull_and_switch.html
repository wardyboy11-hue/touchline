Getting the voice ready

Set up

  • Blue have 7 players (B1 to B7) against Red 5 (R1 to R5) and a Red keeper.
  • B6 and B7 stay high and wide as the finishers.
  • Two wide channels are marked, the pull side and the switch side.

Rules

  • Blue must make at least three passes on one side before they can switch.
  • Blue can only shoot after a switch of play.
  • Red score by winning the ball and passing it to a neutral target on the halfway line.
  • Swap which side is the pull side each round.

How it starts and restarts

  • Blue start with the ball deep, Red defend with five
  • Ball out of play or save: Blue restart from B1.
  • Red win it: play to the target, then restart.

Coaching points

  • Pull first: draw them in with short passes, then hit the space.
  • Look before you receive: where is the free player?
  • Hit it early and with pace. A slow switch gives them time to slide.
  • Stay wide and high: the far player must wait and be patient.

Progressions

  • Make it 7v6 so Red can recover faster.
  • Switch must be played in under two touches.
  • Play with two goals: score after a switch and you get double.
