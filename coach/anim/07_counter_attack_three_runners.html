Getting the voice ready

For parents: what to look for

Watch the moment we win the ball: the first pass goes forward through a gap, and three players sprint straight away.

Set up

  • Red start with the ball in our half.
  • Our 3-5-2 defends; when we win it we counter.

Rules

  • Goal within 8 seconds of winning it counts double.

How it starts and restarts

  • Red have the ball in our half
  • Ball out: Red restart from halfway.

Coaching points

  • First look forward.
  • Pass through the gap between two opponents.
  • Three runners: left, middle, right.

Progressions

  • Red recover with two players.
  • Limit to five passes.
