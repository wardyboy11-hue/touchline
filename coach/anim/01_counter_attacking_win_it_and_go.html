Getting the voice ready

Set up

  • Blue 4 attackers (B1 to B4) against Red 4 defenders (R1 to R4) and a Red keeper at the right hand goal.
  • Red outlet players R5 and R6 wait on the halfway line either side of the area.
  • Blue recovery defenders B5 and B6 wait near the left hand goal with a Blue keeper.

Rules

  • Blue have eight touches or one minute to shoot.
  • Red can only counter once they have won the ball or the keeper has made a save.
  • A goal for Red inside ten seconds of winning the ball counts double.
  • Offside applies in the right hand half only.

How it starts and restarts

  • Blue start with the ball on the halfway line and attack the right hand goal
  • Ball out of play or a goal: Blue restart from halfway with a new ball.
  • If Blue score, the keeper restarts to Red outlet players as before.

Coaching points

  • Go early: the first pass after the regain is forward and positive.
  • Run in three lanes: one wide left, one wide right, one through the middle.
  • The player in the middle arrives late so the defenders cannot mark them early.
  • Take one touch to get the ball out of your feet, then move it.
  • Defenders: slow the ball down, never dive in, and get goal side.

Progressions

  • Make it 5v4 on the break, then 4v4.
  • Add a six second limit to score from the regain.
  • Play the full 9v9 or 11v11 game and award bonus points for goals from regains.
