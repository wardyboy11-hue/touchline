Getting the voice ready

For parents: what to look for

Watch the keeper start the attack and the defenders spread out wide, then the ball goes forward quickly.

Set up

  • Our keeper starts every attack.

Rules

  • Red can only press after the first pass.

How it starts and restarts

  • Our keeper (1) has the ball
  • Every restart from our keeper.

Coaching points

  • Back three get wide.
  • Eight drops to help.
  • Play out, then go forward fast.

Progressions

  • Red press straight away.
