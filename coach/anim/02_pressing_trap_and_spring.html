Getting the voice ready

Set up

  • Blue 4 pressers (B1 to B4) plus B5 as a spare, against Red 5 (R1 to R5) and a Red keeper (KL).
  • Blue start in a 1 2 1 shape in Red's half. Red start with the keeper on the ball.
  • A second Blue keeper (KR) defends the right hand goal.

Rules

  • Blue score only after winning the ball in the Red half. A goal counts double.
  • Red score by playing through the Blue press and getting a shot at the right hand goal.
  • If Red play long, Blue get a point for the first header or second ball won.
  • Rotate who presses first every two minutes.

How it starts and restarts

  • Red keeper starts with the ball and plays out to a centre back
  • Ball out of play: Red keeper restarts.
  • Blue shoot: the Red keeper restarts straight away, so Blue must reset the trap.

Coaching points

  • Curve the run: show the ball carrier one way and shut the pass behind you.
  • Wait for the trigger: a slow pass, a bad touch or a back pass to the side.
  • Spring as one: when the first player goes, everyone moves with him.
  • Win it high, then go forward at once.

Progressions

  • Add a Red spare player so it is 5v6 and the press must be sharper.
  • Count the seconds from regain to shot. Aim for under six.
  • Play 9v9 and award a point for every regain in the final third.
