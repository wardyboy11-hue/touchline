Getting the voice ready

For parents: what to look for

Watch your child play in different positions. That is the plan: everyone learns every job in the team.

Set up

  • 8v8, our shape is 3-2-1-1 plus keeper (a mini 3-5-2).

Rules

  • Coach whistles every few minutes: players rotate positions.

How it starts and restarts

  • Our keeper starts
  • Keeper restarts.

Coaching points

  • Learn the job of the position you are in.
  • Talk to the teammate you swap with.

Progressions

  • Players choose their own rotation.
