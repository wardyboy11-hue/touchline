Getting the voice ready

For parents: what to look for

Watch what happens the moment we lose the ball: do the closest players chase it straight away together for about five seconds?

Set up

  • Normal game, our 3-5-2.

Rules

  • A win back inside five seconds is a bonus point.

How it starts and restarts

  • We attack, then lose the ball
  • Ball out: keeper restart.

Coaching points

  • React straight away, no sulking.
  • Nearest three press.
  • Cut off the easy pass.
  • After five seconds, drop into shape.

Progressions

  • Bonus only if we score within 10 seconds of winning it back.
