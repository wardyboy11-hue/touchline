Getting the voice ready

Set up

  • Blue defend the left hand goal: back four (B1 to B4), two midfielders (B5, B6) and a keeper.
  • Red have five attackers (R1 to R5) with a ball in midfield.
  • Cones mark a channel 10m wide either side so Blue can see the shift distance.

Rules

  • Red may play anywhere but Blue score points for each win of the ball.
  • Blue get a point for every full shift where all six players move together.
  • Red score by getting a shot on goal from inside the area.
  • Switch roles every four minutes.

How it starts and restarts

  • Red start with the ball in midfield, Blue hold a compact block
  • Blue win the ball: they play it to a target player, then restart from the halfway line.
  • Ball out of play: Red restart from halfway.

Coaching points

  • Shift together: when the ball moves, we all move. Nobody stays behind.
  • Squeeze when it goes back: step up and make the pitch smaller.
  • Hold the line: stay the same distance apart, arms length when you can.
  • Talk early: name the ball player and say who covers.

Progressions

  • Add a sixth Red attacker so Blue must hold with fewer numbers.
  • Make the shift a time limit: the block has to move in three seconds.
  • Play 11v11 and award a point for every compact defensive phase.
