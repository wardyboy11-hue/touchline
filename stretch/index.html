Stay safe
  • Ease in to a gentle pull. Never pain, never force it, no bouncing.
  • Stop if anything feels sharp and tell a coach.
  • Growing? Sore below the kneecap (Osgood-Schlatter) or in the heel (Sever's) means go gentler and tell a coach or your parents.
  • Breathe slowly the whole time.
