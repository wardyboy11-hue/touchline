# Home map, release 111

This sits on the command release. Tabs and routes are unchanged. The landing page is what moved.

## Before

Coach Today opened on the date, then alerts, the next match, the week, training, and Comms. Questions and player voice lived on Comms, several taps down. Parents and players opened Home on the welcome and the next session, then had to hunt fixtures, kit and clips.

A rough tap count from the old home, phone, after sign in:

| Task | Coach | Parent or player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unanswered question | Today, Comms, then the list. Three taps. | Not on the coach phone. |
| Next match | On Today, often below the checklist. | Home, then Games. Two taps. |
| Kit | Open the match, then the kit control. | Games, then the fixture. Two or three. |
| 352 animation | Train, then the animation list. | Not on the home row. |
| Training plan | Train, then Plan. | Home, if the post is published. |

## After

Coach Today leads with questions and player voice, unread count, and a reply that previews the words. Send posts it. Cancel posts nothing. A worry that mentions being hurt, being scared, welfare or abuse is not answered in that list and is not deleted there. It points at Welfare and admin.

Under that is Find, which uses the same command registry as the voice helper. Then the next match and alerts, then the week and training. Comms is still there.

Parent and player Home lead with What is new: news, the next fixtures, and new sessions. Each row is one tap. Mark all read clears the dots on this phone only. Find is the same registry. The five tabs stay Home, Games, Events, Media and Me, with words as well as icons. Desktop coach navigation stays a left rail.

Existing screens are still reached from those tabs. Nothing was removed.

## Guidance used

Nielsen Norman Group, Mobile Navigation Patterns (tab bars for top destinations, search when the set is larger than the tabs): https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-navigation-patterns/

Material Design, navigation bars and one primary destination set: https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-bar/overview

Apple Human Interface Guidelines, tab bars, labels, and 44 point targets: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars
