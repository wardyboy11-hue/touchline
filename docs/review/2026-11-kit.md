# Kit choice, 7 November correction

The family pages were not reading a saved away kit. If a fixture had no kit field, or the kit field was not exactly H, A or T, the page used the venue: away ground became "Away kit", and a home ground became the blue home kit. Setting Home or away on Set match also copied that choice into the kit field.

The 7 November game is away, so the page showed the away kit. A connector is writing the saved kit to blue. This release stops the guess. Blue, or H, still shows the home kit. An empty kit shows "Kit to be confirmed".

Only the coach app has the kit control, on Set match and on the match day alert. Tim and Deano already sign in with the coach codes. Families cannot change it. Opening the alert or pressing Cancel does not change the saved kit, and a kick-off or team alert is not sent until a kit is chosen.

No Apps Script paste is required. The kit is a field on the match document the coach app already syncs. Do not replace the live script.

The filmed tours have not been re-recorded. They may still say that an away game means the away kit.
