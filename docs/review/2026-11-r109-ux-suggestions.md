# Bigger UX suggestions (not built in r109)

These change layout or flow, or need audio or video re-recorded, so they are listed rather than built.

1. **Games first on the Games tab.** Today three explainer cards (Match days, Pre match music, Wake up alarm) sit above the actual games. Older parents scroll past three cards to find Saturday's game. Put the next game first and fold music and alarm into one "Extras" section underneath.
2. **One main button per screen.** Every family screen has the yellow "Share this page" bar AND the yellow Ask button, both in the same corner, and often a yellow card button too. Make Share a small outline button at the top of the page and keep yellow for the one thing we want people to do on that screen.
3. **A proper Back button in the family app.** Tabs work, but sub pages (position guide, FAQ, player card, My data) rely on a "Back" link at the bottom or the phone's own back gesture. A fixed "‹ Back" at the top left of every sub page would help people who do not know the gesture.
4. **First open in three short steps, not one long privacy page.** The first screen is a long privacy notice, then welcome steps, then the alerts prompt. Suggest: (1) "Welcome, this is the team app" with a 30 second video, (2) a short privacy summary with "Read the full version", (3) Turn on alerts. Same content, less reading up front.
5. **Rename Comms to Messages on the coach side.** Plainer, but the coach video guide 04-comms.mp4 and the chapter audio say "Comms", so both need re-recording at the same time.
6. **Training 11/11 words "unit", "back unit", "attack unit", "rondo".** Swap to "back group", "attack group", "keep ball game". This needs 13 voice over clips regenerated (10 whole team, 3 position clips) with ElevenLabs, keeping Deano spelled Deeno. Worth doing for the next session page rather than 11/11 which is tonight.
7. **Position names everywhere, not codes.** The key under the chips helps, but RCB, CDM, LST also appear on match cards and the shape page. A shared "position name" helper (GK → Goalkeeper, RCB → Right centre back) used by every page would remove the codes for families entirely.
8. **Ask help on the session and shape pages.** They are full screen pages with no Ask button. Add the same Ask button (or a "Stuck? Ask Tim in the app" line linking back to Ask) so help is one tap away everywhere.
9. **Text size setting.** A "Bigger text" switch in Me (stored on the phone) for parents who want everything at 18px plus.
10. **Contrast fix on the "Cool down" chip** in the parents Training 11/11 card (measured 2.5 to 1; needs 4.5 to 1).
11. **Coach header row.** With word labels the coach header wraps (More drops to a second line next to the account button). Consider moving "Bright" into More so the row fits on one line on small phones.
12. **Usability check with two real people.** Ask one older parent and one player to do three jobs with no help (say if he can come, find Saturday's kick off, turn on alerts), watch where they get stuck. Ten minutes each tells us more than any checklist.
